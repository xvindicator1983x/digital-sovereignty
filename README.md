# Digital Sovereignty

A collection of manifests, guides, and tools for personal digital autonomy — self-hosted infrastructure, privacy, and freedom from surveillance.

## Contents

- **[The Message](manifesto-public.pdf)** — A two-page leaflet on waking up to the surveillance machine and reclaiming your digital life. Signed by *One Who Woke Up*.
- **[Manifesto (Private)](Manifesto.md)** — The full, unredacted manifesto: "The Reckoning." First-person account of the journey from corporate employee to digital sovereign.
- **Guides** — Practical how-tos for building your own self-hosted stack.

## Philosophy

The System runs on money, data extraction, and surveillance. It profits by convincing people they're free while building digital cages. Big Tech and government surveillance are two sides of the same coin.

We fight back with:
- **Local AI** — Run models on your own hardware. No API calls, no telemetry.
- **Self-hosting** — Your data, your servers, your rules.
- **Encryption** — End-to-end for everything.
- **Open standards** — No vendor lock-in.
